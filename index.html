Principles

A few rules that keep the work focused and durable.

1

Let structure carry the meaning.

Strong composition beats decoration. Users should understand the shape of the work before they read every line.

2

Keep the system reusable.

The same components, variables, and interaction patterns should support future pages without another rewrite.

3

Ship with restraint.

Motion, texture, and color should clarify the message, not pull attention away from it.
