About

Practical design for people working inside messy systems.

Brian Kramer works at the intersection of product, operations, and AI surfaces, with a bias toward things that are understandable, useful, and easy to hand off.

Approach

Start with the user decision.

  • Reduce ambiguity before adding features.
  • Make the next action obvious.
  • Keep the system easy to explain.

What matters

Useful, maintainable, and calm.

  • Interfaces that stay readable under pressure.
  • Workflows that survive real handoffs.
  • Components that can be reused without regret.
